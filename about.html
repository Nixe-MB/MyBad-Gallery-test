About

This website is a collection of all MyBad’s photographs. The photos you see here are all small, ordinary moments I did not want to let slip away: a street at golden hour, a cat who decided I was worth a look, a plate of food that deserved a picture, a few friends in ridiculous costumes.

Nothing here is staged. Most of it was taken with whatever camera happened to be in my hands that day, and plenty of it is a little crooked, a little grainy, or not quite in focus. I have made peace with that. I would rather keep an honest moment than a perfect one.

Why “MyBad”?

It is simply the name I go by here, and a small joke at my own expense. Not every shot lands. Some are blurry, some are accidents, and a few of my favourites are both.

How this little place works

  • Every time you visit, the prints land in a different order, on different paper, as if the pile had been tossed on a desk again. Press “Mix them up” if you would like to toss it yourself.
  • Tap the little heart on anything that stays with you. It will wait for you under “Kept close”, on this device only.
  • Open a photo to see it full size. Click it to zoom, and use the arrow keys or a swipe to wander on to the next one.
  • The soft sounds and the floating bits are there for company. If they are too much, “Sound” and “Motion” each have a switch at the top.

A small request

These pictures mean a lot to me. Please do not copy, repost or reuse them without asking first. A kind message goes a long way, and I will almost always say yes.
